# Brand feedback after round 1

*Open this after your round 1 run. Marcus collected it from the five clients on Tuesday.*

These are new rules and preferences. None of them were in the briefs before. They apply from round 2 on, and
to everything we run after you hand in. The briefs in `brands/` stay the source of truth for rates, bonuses and
sample rules. Where a note adds to a brief, follow both.

## Kettle & Crumb (Dana)

"Your messages keep naming Crunchwell and Popsmith back to creators, even when the creator brought them up. Please
don't name a competitor in a message to a creator, ever. Answer the question without the name."

## Tidewater Coffee (Nina)

"When a creator haggles on the rate, don't sweeten it with a second product or an extra sample. One sample per
creator per product is the rule. Offer them a good filming idea instead."

## Brave Paws (Owen)

"If a creator says their dog got sick, threw up, had an upset tummy, itched, or seemed off after one of our products,
it goes to Nora, even when the creator says it's probably unrelated. Send no new product until she clears it. Don't
tell the creator it wasn't our product. Thank them and tell them Nora will be in touch."

## Fernhill Skin (Jules)

"Creators miss the bonus because 'within 12 days of delivery' means nothing to them. Give the bonus deadline as a
calendar date, for example 'by Fri 20 Nov'. Work it out from the delivery date and the current brief."

## Lumen Home (Grace)

"Never call a product natural, all natural or chemical free, never say it burns clean, and never say a scent smells
clean. Creators can say cozy, warm glow and hand poured. Those words test well for us."
