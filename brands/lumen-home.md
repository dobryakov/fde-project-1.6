# Lumen Home: affiliate brief

*Brightline · v3 · Oct 2026 · home fragrance*

## The product

Lumen Home makes candles and home fragrance. The hero product is the **Candle Trio** ($42). The line:

- Candle Trio
- Linen Room Spray
- Reed Diffuser

## Commission and bonus

| | Rate | Who qualifies |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Open rate | 10% | Anyone who joins through the open affiliate link in TikTok Shop Affiliate Center. No sample needed. |
| Targeted rate | 16% | Creators averaging 9,000 or more views per video over the last 30 days |

**Posting bonus:** $70 for 2 videos posted within 14 days of delivery. Paid after the second video goes live. Each video must follow the rules below.

## Samples

- Minimum 8,000 followers.
- Minimum average of 3,000 views per video over the last 30 days.
- One sample per creator per product.
- No new sample while a previous sample is still unposted 21 days after delivery.
- Samples ship within 5 business days of approval, to the US and Canada only. They usually arrive 5 to 8 days after shipping.
- Creators below the minimums can still join the open affiliate link, earn the open rate and ask again once they pass.

## What a video must include

- The product on screen in the first 3 seconds.
- The shop link tagged.
- Posted within 14 days of delivery.
- Any lit candle on camera is attended.

## What creators may never claim

Never say "non-toxic", "clean air" or "purifies". No sleep claims and no wellness claims of any kind. Safe wording: the scent, the warm light, how it looks in a room, "my go-to for a tidy shelf".

## What we never do

- No flat fees.
- No paying for posts.
- No exclusivity. Creators can post any other brand they like.

## Who handles what we can't

- Damaged or missing orders: ops@brightline.example. Ops follows up with the creator within one business day.
- Address changes: ops@brightline.example.
- Anything legal: the account manager, Marcus.

## Tone

Calm, tidy and plain. Use the creator's first name. One emoji at most. Reply in the creator's language. Give one concrete filming idea per reply, not a list.
