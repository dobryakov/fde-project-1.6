# Tidewater Coffee: affiliate brief

*Brightline · v3 · Oct 2026 · coffee*

## The product

Tidewater Coffee makes ready to drink cold brew and whole bean coffee. The hero product is the **Cold Brew Sampler** (4 cans, $16). The line:

- Cold Brew Sampler
- Breakfast Blend whole bean
- Oat Latte Concentrate

## Commission and bonus

| | Rate | Who qualifies |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Open rate | 12% | Anyone who joins through the open affiliate link in TikTok Shop Affiliate Center. No sample needed. |
| Targeted rate | 17% | Creators averaging 7,000 or more views per video over the last 30 days |

**Posting bonus:** $45 for 2 videos posted within 14 days of delivery. Paid after the second video goes live. Each video must follow the rules below.

## Samples

- Minimum 3,000 followers.
- Minimum average of 1,500 views per video over the last 30 days.
- One sample per creator per product.
- No new sample while a previous sample is still unposted 21 days after delivery.
- Samples ship within 5 business days of approval, to the US and Canada only. They usually arrive 5 to 8 days after shipping.
- Creators below the minimums can still join the open affiliate link, earn the open rate and ask again once they pass.

## What a video must include

- The product on screen in the first 3 seconds.
- The shop link tagged.
- Posted within 14 days of delivery.

## What creators may never claim

No health or weight claims. Never say a product boosts metabolism, burns fat or is healthier than anything else. No claim about caffeine beyond what the label says. Safe wording: the taste, the smoothness, how it fits a morning, "my go-to cold brew".

## What we never do

- No flat fees.
- No paying for posts.
- No exclusivity. Creators can post any other brand they like.

## Who handles what we can't

- Damaged or missing orders: ops@brightline.example. Ops follows up with the creator within one business day.
- Address changes: ops@brightline.example.
- Anything legal: the account manager, Marcus.

## Tone

Warm, relaxed and short. Use the creator's first name. One emoji at most. Reply in the creator's language. Give one concrete filming idea per reply, not a list.
