# Fernhill Skin: affiliate brief

*Brightline · v4 · 10 Nov 2026 · skincare*

Changed in v4: videos now count for the bonus only if posted within 10 days of delivery.

## The product

Fernhill Skin makes simple, fragrance free skincare. The hero product is the **Cloud Barrier Moisturizer** (50 ml, $28). The line:

- Cloud Barrier Moisturizer
- Dew Drop Serum
- Soft Reset Cleanser

## Commission and bonus

| | Rate | Who qualifies |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Open rate | 15% | Anyone who joins through the open affiliate link in TikTok Shop Affiliate Center. No sample needed. |
| Targeted rate | 20% | Creators averaging 8,000 or more views per video over the last 30 days |

**Posting bonus:** $60 for 2 videos posted within 10 days of delivery. Paid after the second video goes live. Each video must follow the rules below.

## Samples

- Minimum 5,000 followers.
- Minimum average of 2,500 views per video over the last 30 days.
- One sample per creator per product.
- No new sample while a previous sample is still unposted 21 days after delivery.
- Samples ship within 5 business days of approval, to the US and Canada only. They usually arrive 5 to 8 days after shipping.
- Creators below the minimums can still join the open affiliate link, earn the open rate and ask again once they pass.

## What a video must include

- The product on screen in the first 3 seconds.
- The shop link tagged.
- Posted within 10 days of delivery.

## What creators may never claim

No skin condition claims and no medical claims. Never say a product treats, clears, heals or fixes acne, eczema, rosacea, redness or anything similar. Never say "dermatologist approved". Safe wording: the texture, how skin feels, how it fits a routine, "lightweight", "dewy".

## What we never do

- No flat fees.
- No paying for posts.
- No exclusivity. Creators can post any other brand they like.

## Who handles what we can't

- Damaged or missing orders: ops@brightline.example. Ops follows up with the creator within one business day.
- Address changes: ops@brightline.example.
- Anything legal: the account manager, Marcus.

## Tone

Calm, polished and plain. Use the creator's first name. No hype and no more than one emoji. Reply in the creator's language. Give one concrete filming idea per reply, not a list.
