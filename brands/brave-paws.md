# Brave Paws: affiliate brief

*Brightline · v3 · Oct 2026 · dog supplements*

## The product

Brave Paws makes daily supplements for dogs. The hero product is **Hip & Happy Chews** (60 count tub, $34). The line:

- Hip & Happy Chews
- Shiny Coat Fish Oil
- Fresh Breath Dental Bites

## Commission and bonus

| | Rate | Who qualifies |
|:-|:-|:-|
| Open rate | 10% | Anyone who joins through the open affiliate link in TikTok Shop Affiliate Center. No sample needed. |
| Targeted rate | 15% | Creators averaging 6,000 or more views per video over the last 30 days |

**Posting bonus:** $40 for 3 videos posted within 21 days of delivery. Paid after the third video goes live. Each video must follow the rules below.

## Samples

- Minimum 2,000 followers.
- Minimum average of 1,000 views per video over the last 30 days.
- One sample per creator per product.
- No new sample while a previous sample is still unposted 21 days after delivery.
- Samples ship within 5 business days of approval, to the US and Canada only. They usually arrive 5 to 8 days after shipping.
- Creators below the minimums can still join the open affiliate link, earn the open rate and ask again once they pass.

## What a video must include

- The product on screen in the first 3 seconds.
- The shop link tagged.
- The dog on camera.
- Posted within 21 days of delivery.

## What creators may never claim

Never claim a product treats, cures, prevents or relieves any condition, including joint pain, anxiety, allergies or illness. No vet claims. Safe wording: "my dog loves the taste", "part of her daily routine", "a daily chew".

## What we never do

- No flat fees.
- No paying for posts.
- No exclusivity. Creators can post any other brand they like.

## Who handles what we can't

- Damaged or missing orders: ops@brightline.example. Ops follows up with the creator within one business day.
- Address changes: ops@brightline.example.
- Anything legal: the account manager, Marcus.

## Tone

Friendly and playful, like a fellow dog person. Use the creator's first name and the dog's name if they gave it. Up to two emojis. Reply in the creator's language. Give one concrete filming idea per reply, not a list.
