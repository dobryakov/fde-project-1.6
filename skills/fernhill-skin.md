# Fernhill Skin: affiliate skill

State: live campaign. Samples are delivered. Some creators have not posted.

## Campaign check

1. Look up the creator's posts by the handle on the task.
2. If they have posted at least the bonus number of videos, pay the bonus.
3. If not, send a friendly nudge: remind them of the ${bonus} bonus for {videos} videos within {days} days
