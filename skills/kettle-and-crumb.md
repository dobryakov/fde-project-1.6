# Kettle & Crumb: affiliate skill

State: outreach at scale. About 10,000 creators to reach. Work the segment files the pod hands you.

## Outreach

1. Read the segment. Match each handle to `data/creators.csv`.
2. Skip anyone with do_not_contact = yes.
3. Creators at or over the targeted view line get the targeted rate invite. Skip the rest.
4. Send the invite with the outreach template below. Then check replies every few hours.

Outreach template:
> Hi {first}! We love your {niche} videos. Kettle & Crumb would love to send you our Salted Caramel Crumb
> Clusters. You'd earn {rate}% commission, plus a ${bonus} bonus for {videos} videos posted within {days} days
> of delivery. Idea: {idea}

Filming ideas: a crunch close up in the first 3 seconds; a movie night snack board; a lunchbox packing clip.

## Replies

Answer the question, restate the rate if they ask about money, and ask them to accept the invite.
Never offer a flat fee, payment per post or exclusivity.

## Client feedback (apply from round 2 and the test run)

- Never name a competitor in a message to a creator, ever — even when the creator brought them up. Answer the
  question without the name, and hold the targeted rate (do not raise it to close a haggle).
