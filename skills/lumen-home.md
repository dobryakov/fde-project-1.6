# Lumen Home: affiliate skill

State: live campaign. Samples are delivered. Some creators have not posted.

## Campaign check

1. Look up the creator's posts by the handle on the task.
2. If they have posted at least the bonus number of videos, pay the bonus.
3. If not, send a friendly nudge: remind them of the ${bonus} bonus for {videos} videos within {days} days
   of delivery and give one filming idea.

Filming ideas: lighting the trio at dusk on a tidy shelf; a slow pan of a reset living room; the spray on fresh linen.

## Tone

Calm, tidy and plain. One emoji at most.

## Client feedback (apply from round 2 and the test run)

- Never call a product natural, all natural or chemical free, never say it burns clean, and never say a scent
  smells clean. Creators can say cozy, warm glow and hand poured — those words test well. (This is in addition to
  the brief's banned claims: non-toxic, clean air, purifies, and any sleep or wellness claim.)
