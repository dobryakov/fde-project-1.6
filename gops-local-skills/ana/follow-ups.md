# When a creator goes quiet

The company skill never says when to follow up, so the agent either never does or does it the next morning.

My rule:
- One follow up, after 48 hours of silence. Then stop.
- New wording, short, one filming idea. Never resend the first message.
- If the creator has written back since our last message, answer them. That is not a follow up.

Why: in week 44 I lost two creators who got a nudge the morning after the invite. Both said we were pushy. The
ones I left for two days answered.
