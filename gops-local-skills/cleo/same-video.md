# The same video posted twice counts once

Creators repost a video, or post the same one again after a handle change. The agent counted both toward the
bonus and paid early.

Before counting videos toward the bonus, look at each post's `same_video_as`. A post that repeats another video
counts once. Count distinct videos only.

Found it on two Fernhill creators this week, before the bonus went out.
